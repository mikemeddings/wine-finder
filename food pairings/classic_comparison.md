# Classic pairings: Dornenburg & Page vs the app

Source: *What to Drink with What You Eat* (Dornenburg & Page, 2006), Chapter 5 (food → drink, 518 entries) and Chapter 6 (drink → food, 230 entries), parsed from the book with its emphasis kept: plain = recommended, **bold** = frequently recommended, CAPS = very highly recommended, *CAPS = classic, plus each entry's AVOID list. Wine names were mapped to the app's grapes (e.g. Chianti → Sangiovese, Beaujolais → Gamay, Sancerre → Sauvignon Blanc, Barolo → Nebbiolo) and dishes to the app's ten foods using the app's own food words. Note: the app counts duck as Chicken, so duck pairings land there.

App column = share of that grape's red/white wines (all stores) that the app currently tags as a classic match for the food.

| Result | Grape × food pairs |
|---|---|
| Book recommends it (bold or stronger) and the app agrees | 134 |
| Book rates it very highly or classic, the app doesn't tag it | 38 |
| Book lists it plainly once or twice, app doesn't tag it (adventurous candidates) | 51 |
| App tags it, book only lists it plainly | 25 |
| App tags it, book doesn't mention it | 15 |
| Neither | 137 |
